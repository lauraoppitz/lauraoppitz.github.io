01 DREAMING IN COLOUR, 2026

This book is an aim to translate dreams and its fluid, merged spaces into paper. Transparent pages carry digital collages that overlay on top of each other, so places, memories, and feelings of dreams bleed into each other as you turn the page. The source of the visual imagery in this book was captured with a digital camera, while characters were added in Blender. Post-production in Photoshop.

02 TWO MONTHS OFF, 2026

This music video was inspired by "Two Months Off" by Underworld, and is an exploration of the relationship between sound and generative, reactive visuals. To achieve this, I created, then layered various visuals in Touchdesigner, making it audio reactive to fit the music.

03 NORTH SEA JAZZ, 2025

Poster, 70 x 100 cm. Made for North Sea Jazz festival, awarded with third place in their yearly poster competition announced to WdKA students. I collected sea shells from the north sea, which was turned into a miniature version of a headphone. After photographing this object, I turned it into a poster and t-shirt design.

04 PUTYU SEF, 2026

Album cover art, made with the technique of reduction linocut. This work was a collaboration, I was responsible for the illustration and digital post-production, while typography was my collaborator’s role. The illustration itself is a visualization of the lyrics found in the Single, that fits the band’s fusion punk aesthetic in style.

05 A DREAMSCAPE, 2026

Created in Blender. Inspired by dreamscapes, I aimed to recreate a space I dreamed about as detailed and as precisely as possible. This exercise challenged my ability to stick to exact visions, and to make myself remember a specific, though imaginary space in detail.
